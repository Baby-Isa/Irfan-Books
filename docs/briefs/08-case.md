**Title:** The stages of the journey
**Serves:** B3, Part Three "The Road" (`docs/ARGUMENT.md`; `docs/ladders/00-book-chain.md`)

**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** Since God is already near, the journey covers no distance: it removes veils, in four stages, in order.

## Chapter points
- **8.1** God is already near, so the journey covers no distance; it removes veils inside us.
- **8.2** The traveller's part comes first, in two stages: polishing the self with the law, then emptying the heart of everything but God.
- **8.3** Once the heart is emptied, God adorns it, and the journey's first stretch ends in passing away; so the four stages come in a fixed order.
