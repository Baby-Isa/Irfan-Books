**Title:** Love, the engine
**Serves:** B3, Part Three "The Road" (`docs/ARGUMENT.md`; `docs/ladders/00-book-chain.md`)

**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** Discipline alone runs dry; love carries the traveller on, until even love is left at the door.

## Chapter points
- **11.1** Fear and renunciation can start the journey, but love carries it.
- **11.2** Love is God's gift, so the Imams asked for it, and worship from love is the worship of the free.
- **11.3** Love too is a means, so at the Beloved even love is left behind.
