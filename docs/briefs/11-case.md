**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** The third stage, adornment, is God's gift to the emptied heart: knowledge and light, and love, which carries the traveller until even love is left at the door.

## Chapter points
- **11.1** Once the heart is emptied, God adorns it with gifts no effort makes, briefly at first.
- **11.2** Among those gifts is love, which the Imams asked for, and worship from love is the worship of the free.
- **11.3** Love too is a means, so at the Beloved even love is left behind.
