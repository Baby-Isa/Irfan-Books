## The approved outline for this chapter (`docs/outline/part-2.md`)
#### 6. No path outside the law
**Subheading:** So inward a journey is kept safe by the shariʿa: law, path and truth are one thing at three depths, so there is no inner path outside the law.
**Conclusion:** there is no inner path outside the shariʿa. **Serves:** B2 **Old number:** 6
**Opener:** Standing for prayer, we say *Allahu akbar*, and "Suddenly, you think about did I lock my car?" (L06 ¶30). The rules are met; something else is missing. The chapter names both and shows they are one. Epigraph [6-M]: "who do not fail to show You their gratitude and do not take Your orders lightly" [@light, p. 166] — Arabic held §126–127.
**Words you'll meet:** *shariʿa* · *tariqa* · *haqiqa* · *ihsan* · *karamat*
**Sections:**
1. ## Shell, core and kernel [6.1] — law, path and truth are one reality at three depths.
   - Ship, sea, pearl: the pearl "cannot be found except by sailing through the sea, and you cannot sail without a ship" (L06 ¶13–17; credited by the Sheikh to Najm al-Din Kubra [VERIFY Fawaʾih]; not `inner_secrets` p. 50, which inverts it). Prologue's "the beginning of ʿirfan is the shariʿa" recalled in words only.
   - Mulla ʿAbd al-Samad Hamadani: "Sharia is a shell tariqa is a core and haqiqa is the kernel." (L06 ¶23). Mutahhari beside it: "the kernel of the kernel" [@light, p. 15] (footnote only).
   - In salah: serving, drawing near, arriving (khidma, qurb, wusul) (L06 ¶24). The saying "The shari`ah is my words, tariqah my actions, haqiqah my states" (L06 ¶13; [@inner_secrets, p. 32]) — Arabic: n.a.
   - Certainty in three degrees, the Paris image (L06 ¶43–44); Islam, iman, ihsan, with Q 49:14, "faith has not yet entered into your hearts" (L06 ¶45–48). Footnote (Amuli): "shari`ah is possible without tariqah although tariqah is not possible without shari`ah" [@inner_secrets, p. 48].
2. ## The inner never cancels the outer [6.2] — wonders without the law prove nothing, and the first inner work is attention in prayer.
   - Kubra's rule: whoever flies or walks on water yet abandons "even one injunction of God", "you should know that they are liars" (L06 ¶17–20). Karama versus sihr (¶17–18).
   - Bahari: jurisprudence is "an introductory means" to good character, and character to knowledge of tawhid (L06 ¶21–22).
   - Khomeini: "Without adhering to the apparent teachings of Islam it is not possible that the light of the Divine Knowledge could descend upon his heart" [@forty_hadith, p. 35]. Footnote (Amuli): "Whoever does not protect his spiritual state and path by the shari`ah will destroy that state" [@inner_secrets, p. 42].
   - Forty days of hunger are easy; "continuously practicing Sharia is difficult" (L06 ¶65–70), as the Sheikh's own caution (D15; see Cut).
   - Attention: "Attention is very elusive" (L06 ¶27–33). Khomeini: "How is it that the study of a book often absorbs man's entire attention, but prayer does not?" [@light, p. 135].
3. ## To the last breath [6.3] — even the end of the road is lived under the law, among people.
   - Tabatabaʾi: "from the beginning of wayfaring to its final stage the wayfarer must abide by all the precepts of the luminous Shari'ah"; the claim of release is "a big lie and slander" [@kernel, p. 30].
   - The Prophet, asked why he strove in worship though forgiven (Q 48:2): "Don't you want me to be God's grateful servant?" [@kernel, p. 30]. (Ties to the epigraph.)
   - The four journeys are previewed in a sentence or two, no more: to God, in God, back to creation, among creation (L09 ¶70–72). The last journey ends among people. Pointer to Ch 16, which walks them (FRAMEWORK §8.1).
   - (L09 ¶89 "of course, they follow the Sharia" and L09 ¶91 "would bring him down to the level of ordinary people": moved to Ch 16.)
**Boxes:** Word Study: *karamat*, *muʿjiza* and *sihr* (L06 ¶17–18). (The four-journeys table box is cut, FRAMEWORK §8.1.)
**Try this:** For one prayer a day this week, pray it at the start of its time with nothing scheduled around it, as Khomeini advises ("set aside a certain time out of his day and night hours exclusively for the sake of worship" [@forty_hadith, p. 375]); at *Allahu akbar*, "know what you are saying" (L06 ¶26).
**Hand-off:** The law is the boundary. Who walks beside the traveller?
