**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** In the fourth stage, love ends in losing the false self before God's glory, and in coming back sober, seeing God in all things while remaining oneself.

## Chapter points
- **12.1** In annihilation the traveller loses the sense of self before God's glory; he neither ceases to exist nor becomes God, so what is lost is a false self.
- **12.2** Annihilation comes in three degrees, each the lived truth of words we already say.
- **12.3** Annihilation is a beginning, not an end: the healthy outcome is to come back to oneself, sober, seeing God and creation together.
