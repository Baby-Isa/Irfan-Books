## The test for every step
- It uses only what earlier steps (or earlier sections, or earlier chapters' points) established, and its key words already appear there. "So" or "therefore" only where the earlier step really gives it.
- One numbered step per body paragraph (boxes and displayed verses do not count; a story box can be noted in italics where it sits). Where several paragraphs explain things the argument needs before it can be made, they are nested steps (a, b, c), and the prose must say before the first what is coming ("The Qur'an names three…").
- The section points, read in order, form a chain that proves the chapter point. The last step of each section lands its point.
- Short plain claims a reader could repeat, not descriptions.
