# Adding the Sheikh's stories to Chapters One to Seven (D47)

The user's uncle, who knows Sheikh Bahmanpour, says the Sheikh's stories "are the best bit". The audit (`reviews/stories-audit.md`) found 61 stories in the lectures, and Chapters One to Seven tell almost none of them. The user wants one or two per chapter **where they hit the quality mark**.

## The quality mark
A story goes in only if all of these hold:
- it **carries the point of the section** it sits in (the reader finishes it knowing that point better);
- the transcript tells it **clearly** (read the ¶ range in `transcripts/text/IrfanNN.md`; if a detail is garbled or uncertain, leave the detail out; if the story itself is unclear, choose another — D39);
- its point is **not a joke**, and it has no wry aside (CLAUDE.md: no jokes, ever);
- it is told in the book's voice (TONE 38: plain sentences; no suspense, no tidy closing line).
If no story in the audit meets the mark for your chapter, add none and say why. One strong story beats two thin ones.

## How to tell it
- **As the Sheikh's account:** "Sheikh Bahmanpour tells of…", "In one of his lectures the Sheikh recalled…". The lecture itself is the source; no printed source is needed. Count it towards the Sheikh-naming cap (5 per 1,000 words).
- **No invented detail.** Names, places and words come from the transcript. Do not add dialogue the Sheikh does not give. A hadith or verse inside the story: quote it only from a held extract (`sources/extracts/`, `sources/quran/`), otherwise paraphrase; never write Arabic from memory.
- **Format:** a `::: {.box-story title="Story: …"}` box of 100–200 words, ending on the point it makes for this section (one sentence, not an aphorism). LESSONS 17: one callout box per section plus Try this, so if the section already has a box, either tell the story as a short prose paragraph (it then needs its own step in the ladder) or put it in a section without a box. Do not displace a box the user has approved unless the story is clearly stronger; say so if you do.
- **Mark it for the user:** add a last line to that section's `::: {.point}` note: `New story (D47): <title>, L<NN> ¶<a–b>.` The draft PDF shows it.
- Do not edit `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (others are working in parallel); list any new term.
