**Title:** The five disciplines
**Serves:** B3, Part Three "The Road" (`docs/ARGUMENT.md`; `docs/ladders/00-book-chain.md`)

**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** Once the soul has turned, five disciplines loosen the body's hold on it, each in a measured dose.

## Chapter points
- **10.1** Five disciplines train the soul by loosening the body's hold on it, and the question for each is how much.
- **10.2** Silence, hunger and the night prayer loosen the body's hold where it is strongest: at the tongue, the stomach and sleep.
- **10.3** Seclusion turns the heart away from distraction, and remembrance turns it towards God.
- **10.4** Each discipline is taken in a dose measured to the person, small and kept up.
