**Title:** Emptying: the five disciplines
**Serves:** B3, Part Three "The Road" (`docs/ARGUMENT.md`; `docs/ladders/00-book-chain.md`)

**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** The second stage, emptying, clears the heart of everything but God, and the five disciplines are its tools, each in a measured dose.

## Chapter points
- **10.1** ʿIrfan proper begins with emptying the heart, and five disciplines are its tools.
- **10.2** Silence, hunger and the night prayer shut the doors through which the world fills the heart: the tongue, the stomach and sleep.
- **10.3** Seclusion separates the heart from what distracts it, and remembrance dusts off what remains.
- **10.4** Each discipline is taken in a dose measured to the person, small and kept up.
