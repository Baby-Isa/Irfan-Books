**Title:** Back among people
**Serves:** B5, Part Five "The Destination" (`docs/ARGUMENT.md`; `docs/ladders/00-book-chain.md` line 16)

**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** The completed journey comes back to people, with God: losing the self ends only the first of four journeys, the last two return the traveller to creation to guide it, and the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as) walked them to the end, as far as Karbala.

## Chapter points
- **16.1** Losing the self ends only the first of four journeys; in the second, God takes the traveller through His names and gives him back to himself as His servant.
- **16.2** The road back has two more journeys: the third returns that servant to creation, seen now as God's names at work, and the fourth, which the lectures describe in the Prophet ﷺ, keeps him among people, with God, to guide them.
- **16.3** The fullest form of the return belongs first to the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as), and the rest of us are called to follow them as far as God wills.
- **16.4** Imam al-Husayn (as) shows the return in one life: before God among his people at ʿArafah, and with them and for them at Karbala.

## Sections
1. **A quarter of the way** [16.1]
   - **Point:** Losing the self ends only the first of four journeys; in the second, God takes the traveller through His names and gives him back to himself as His servant.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. The mystics count four journeys (L09 ¶70–72; `light` p. 49). → 2. The first is the road walked so far, and ends in annihilation, "one quarter of the way" (L09 ¶72–75). → 3. "By God": the traveller is under God's guardianship, which the witr prayer asks for in the reader's own words (L09 ¶72, ¶79–80; Salat al-Layl §162; L15 ¶60). → 4. In the second, God shows him His names, as He taught Adam, all but those kept for God and for those above him, and no one is above the Prophet ﷺ (Q 2:31; L09 ¶80–81; `light` p. 49; L15 ¶59). → 5. It ends in remaining, the traveller "a servant of Allah" again: Ch 12's sober return, placed at the end of the second journey (L09 ¶81). → 6. Therefore the point.
2. **The way back** [16.2]
   - **Point:** The road back has two more journeys: the third returns that servant to creation, seen now as God's names at work, and the fourth, which the lectures describe in the Prophet ﷺ, keeps him among people, with God, to guide them.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. Third: back to creation under God's guardianship; the three worlds seen as His names at work (L09 ¶83). → 2. Its fruit is certitude (Q 6:75; L09 ¶85). → 3. Certitude is for telling others; the Sheikh now calls him the knower; Mutahhari: "in order to guide the people" (L09 ¶85–86; `light` pp. 49–50). → 4. Fourth: how everything returns to God; "amongst the people while still united with God" (L09 ¶87–88; `light` p. 50). → 5. Can he live among people again, and will they pull him away? (a) at the first journey's end he cannot see them: the gnostic "did not see them" (`light` p. 63; L09 ¶91); (b) the fourth brings him "down to the level of ordinary people", and he sees God in them (L09 ¶91). → 6. Outwardly among people, inwardly with God: the epigraph in words; story box. → 7. Therefore the point.
3. **Who completes it** [16.3]
   - **Point:** The fullest form of the return belongs first to the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as), and the rest of us are called to follow them as far as God wills.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. The third is open to high-calibre mystics, the fourth to the Prophet and his successors (L09 ¶87, ¶89). → 2. In the Imams: inner knowledge, yet "of course, they follow the Sharia" (L09 ¶89). → 3. Is holding both worlds really theirs alone? Side by side, Tabatabaʾi: "perhaps" the prophets', the awliyaʾ's "and whoever God wishes"; the Prophet's states beyond any archangel (a saying the *Kernel* quotes) and "I am just a human being like you" (`kernel` p. 20; Q 18:110). → 4. Not closed: *wilaya* is "not at all exclusive"; the Prophet is an exemplar (`kernel` p. 20; Q 33:21). → 5. For us now: stay in society at every stage (L15 ¶67). → 6. Therefore: common ground (both place the fullest form of the return first in the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as); both leave the way open as far as God gives) and the point.
4. **From ʿArafah to Karbala** [16.4]
   - **Point:** Imam al-Husayn (as) shows the return in one life: before God among his people at ʿArafah, and with them and for them at Karbala.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. What does the whole way look like in a life we know? Khomeini points to one: the prophets "established justice… rose against the wrongdoers. Imam Husayn also did the same thing. Just see his Supplication of the Day of Arafah" (`light` p. 138). → 2. The scene: among his household, hands raised "like a poor man begging food" (Mafatih p. 374). → 3. What he asked: "make me fear You as if I can see You" (ʿArafah §182). → 4. The book's reflection (D16): martyred on ʿAshura with his companions at Karbala (Mafatih p. 401), for them on Khomeini's reading. → 5. Therefore (section and chapter), then stop on the concrete: "express sympathy to each other" (Mafatih p. 401).
