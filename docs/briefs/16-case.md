**Conclusion (one sentence the chapter proves):** The completed journey comes back to people, with God: losing the self ends only the first of four journeys, the last two return the traveller to creation to guide it, and the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as) walked them to the end, as far as Karbala.

## Chapter points
- **16.1** Losing the self ends only the first of four journeys; in the second, God takes the traveller through His names and gives him back to himself as His servant.
- **16.2** The third and fourth journeys bring that servant back to people, with God, to guide them.
- **16.3** The whole return belongs to the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as), and the rest of us are called to follow them as far as God wills.
- **16.4** Imam al-Husayn (as) shows the return in one life: before God among his people at ʿArafah, and with them and for them at Karbala.

## Sections
1. **A quarter of the way** [16.1]
   - **Point:** Losing the self ends only the first of four journeys; in the second, God takes the traveller through His names and gives him back to himself as His servant.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. The mystics count four journeys (L09 ¶70–72; `light` p. 49). → 2. The first is the road walked so far, and ends in annihilation, "one quarter of the way" (L09 ¶72–75). → 3. The last three are "by God": the traveller is under God's guardianship (L09 ¶79). → 4. In the second, God shows him His names, as He taught Adam (Q 2:31; L09 ¶80; `light` p. 49; L15 ¶59). → 5. It ends in remaining, the traveller "a servant of Allah" again: Ch 12's sober return, placed (L09 ¶81). → 6. Therefore the point.
2. **The way back** [16.2]
   - **Point:** The third and fourth journeys bring that servant back to people, with God, to guide them.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. Third: back to creation under God's guardianship; creation seen as His names at work (L09 ¶83). → 2. Its fruit is certitude (Q 6:75; L09 ¶85). → 3. Certitude is for telling others; Mutahhari: "in order to guide the people" (L09 ¶85–86; `light` pp. 49–50). → 4. Fourth: how everything returns to God; "amongst the people while still united with God" (L09 ¶87–88; `light` p. 50). → 5. The worry, answered from both ends: (a) at the first journey's end the gnostic "did not see them" (`light` p. 63; L09 ¶91); (b) the fourth brings him "down to the level of ordinary people" (L09 ¶91). → 6. Outwardly among people, inwardly with God: the epigraph in words; story box. → 7. Therefore the point.
3. **Who completes it** [16.3]
   - **Point:** The whole return belongs to the Prophet ﷺ and the Imams (as), and the rest of us are called to follow them as far as God wills.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. The third is open to high-calibre mystics, the fourth to the Prophet and his successors (L09 ¶87, ¶89). → 2. In the Imams: inner knowledge, yet "of course, they follow the Sharia" (L09 ¶89). → 3. Side by side, Tabatabaʾi: holding both worlds is "perhaps" the prophets', the awliyaʾ's "and whoever God wishes"; the Prophet's states beyond any archangel and "I am just a human being like you" (`kernel` p. 20; Q 18:110). → 4. Not closed: *wilaya* is "not at all exclusive"; the Prophet is an exemplar (`kernel` p. 20; Q 33:21). → 5. Common ground, and for us now: stay in society at every stage (L15 ¶67). → 6. Therefore the point.
4. **From ʿArafah to Karbala** [16.4]
   - **Point:** Imam al-Husayn (as) shows the return in one life: before God among his people at ʿArafah, and with them and for them at Karbala.
   - **Paragraph points:** 1. Khomeini: spiritual sciences do not make a man idle; "Imam Husayn also did the same thing. Just see his Supplication of the Day of Arafah" (`light` p. 138). → 2. The scene: among his household, hands raised "like a poor man begging food" (Mafatih p. 374). → 3. What he asked: "make me fear You as if I can see You" (ʿArafah §182). → 4. The book's reflection (D16): martyred on ʿAshura with those martyred with him (Mafatih pp. 400–401). → 5. Therefore (section and chapter), then stop on the concrete: "express sympathy to each other" (Mafatih p. 401).
