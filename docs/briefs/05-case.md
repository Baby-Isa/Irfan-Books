## The approved outline for this chapter (`docs/outline/part-2.md`)
#### 5. The soul and its worlds
**Subheading:** The self is deeper than it looks: it lives in more than one world, its deeds give it a form, and what it finally becomes is in our hands.
**Conclusion:** the self is deeper than it appears, and its final shape is in our hands. **Serves:** B2 **Old number:** 5
**Opener:** At a graveside we say the dead are in *barzakh*, and picture a waiting room somewhere else. The Sheikh: "Exactly we don't go anywhere." (L05 ¶6). Tonight we will sleep, which the tradition calls death's brother. Epigraph [5-M]: "My Lord, if my disregard for preparations to meet You has put me to sleep, my knowledge of Your kind bounties has awakened me." [@light, p. 166] — Arabic held §118–119.
**Words you'll meet:** *ʿalam al-mulk* · *barzakh* / *ʿalam al-mithal* · *ʿalam al-ʿuqul* · *badan mithali* · *muraqaba*
**Sections:**
1. ## Depths, not distances [5.1] — the material, imaginal and intellectual worlds are one reality at three depths.
   - Mulk, mithal/barzakh (shape and colour, no matter), then ʿuqul; "It is another dimension of the same world in which we are living" (L05 ¶3–5; L04 ¶61–66). Kursi, ʿarsh and jabarut in one line only (L04 ¶85–86).
2. ## A soul that crosses them [5.2] — the soul spans the worlds and moves among them in sleep, death and contemplation.
   - Sleep: the soul touches the middle world; dreams that come true (L04 ¶64–68). "Know that sleep is the brother of death." [@lantern, p. 24].
   - Death: "the soul doesn't leave the body, the soul dumps the body" (L04 ¶69); the image body, with "shape and size without the mass" [@self_knowledge, p. 76]; L05 ¶6–9.
   - Contemplation: muraqaba turns attention from the body; "I don't mean that your soul comes out of the body. I just want attention." (L04 ¶70–76). Tabatabaʾi's experience, as the Sheikh reports it (L04 ¶72).
   - Angels have "a known place" (Q 37:164); the soul has no fixed station (L05 ¶7–8).
3. ## The form our deeds make [5.3] — deeds shape an inner form, deformed or luminous, that we will meet.
   - The image body is darkened by sin, brightened by piety (L05 ¶10–14); Q 78:18, "you will come in groups", with the hadith of people raised by their natures [@self_knowledge, p. 21].
   - Khomeini: "there is another form and a different face of him, which is heavenly and invisible, which is determined by the qualities of his soul and his inner nature" [@forty_hadith, p. 41].
   - Q 57:12, "their light moving swiftly in front of them" (the lecture's "56:12" corrected). Shomali: "Sometimes rewards or punishments are nothing other than the actions." [@self_knowledge, p. 77].
4. ## The self can change its state [5.4] — one nafs, three states, and it can move between them.
   - "we don't have different types of nufus": commanding (Q 12:53), self-blaming (Q 75:2), at peace (Q 89:27, named, not quoted) (L05 ¶46–50). Self-blame is a hopeful sign (¶47).
   - Amuli counts four: "commanding to evil", "reproachful", "inspired", "at rest" [@inner_secrets, p. 192] (side by side).
   - What moves it: dhikr (Q 13:28, "The hearts find rest in Allah's remembrance!"), fasting and night prayer as release from the body (L05 ¶51–53, ¶56–57).
   - Close: "whatever we do with it, would remain with us forever" (L05 ¶56).
**Boxes:** Story: Qadi sees himself: "I saw myself standing silent by my side", and finds the mole in the mirror [@kernel, p. 17]. Word Study: *barzakh* (L04 ¶62–63; `self_knowledge` p. 76). Box: "The layers of the soul, counted differently": nafs, qalb, ruh, sirr, khafi [@light, p. 58]; levels "sometimes into seven sections and sometimes into four" [@forty_hadith, p. 32]; L05 ¶31–45 condensed.
**Try this:** Before sleep, take the Lantern's intention: to "disengage the self from its appetites, and to examine yourself by your sleep" [@lantern, p. 24].
