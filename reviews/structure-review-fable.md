# Structure review (Fable), 2026-10-07

Independent developmental review of the 19-chapter journey in `docs/ANALYSIS.md`.
- **Method:** it set nine criteria (arc/naming, progression, accessibility, momentum, practicality, novelty, proportion, fidelity, cultural fit) and scored the proposal against them.
- **Scores:** the original averaged about 3.3/5. The revised five-part, 17-chapter structure averaged about 4.3/5.

**Key findings:**
1. "Map", "journey" and "return" were each used twice.
2. The history was marooned between the emotional peak and the climax.
3. There were forward dependencies:
   - the first journey came before the four journeys
   - Rabiʿa appeared before she was introduced
   - sobriety and intoxication came before Junayd and Bayazid.
4. L16 was split into two unrelated chapters.
5. Chapter 7 was fat (two schemes of the same path).
6. The guide question was answered too late.
7. The controversy was met too late.

**Revised structure:** this was adopted as OUTLINE v2 (see OUTLINE.md and DECISIONS D12).

**Open risks:**
- the mid-book history sag
- Karbala as the fourth journey needs a source and the lecturer's approval
- the "living chain" chapter carries the highest citation risk
- the Khoja/pir box is sensitive
- the Ibn Sina table may grow into an appendix.
