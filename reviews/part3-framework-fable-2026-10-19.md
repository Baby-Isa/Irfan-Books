# Part Three framework: Fable review (2026-10-19)

User's question: how do the two parts (ours/God's), the four stages, the five disciplines and repentance fit together? What is the step-by-step sequence?

- Ch 10's point used Ch 5 vocabulary ("loosen the body's hold") and hid that it is emptying (takhliya).
- Too many unlabelled counts: 4 stages, 4 levels of emptying, 5 disciplines, 3 motives, 3 degrees, 3 reactions.
- Repentance appeared twice (Ch 8 §2, Ch 9 §1); good conduct was never placed in a stage.

Fidelity of one stage per chapter:

- Stage 2 = the five disciplines: a defensible synthesis, not the lecturer's statement (L08/L10 never use the stage names). Support: dhikr dusts off inside the takhliya passage (L07 ¶25–28); silence and hunger guard the heart (L08 ¶50–51); Ibn Sina's riyadah (L15 ¶11, ¶32). Wording: "They are acts the law recommends, so keeping them is still polishing; what makes them emptying's tools is their target, the heart (L07 ¶22)."
- Stage 3 = love: only partly supported. L07 ¶10 + ¶12 and L12 ¶39–41 support "love is among the gifts"; the lecturer's own list for tahliya is visions, inspirations, understanding of the Qur'an (L07 ¶36). Do not write "adornment is love". The fear → renunciation → love motive thread is unstated; keep only as one step resting on the Lantern [LAN58].
- Stage 4: supported (L07 ¶55–59; L09 ¶45–50).
- Hinge line the ladders had omitted: L07 ¶22, tajliya is what ordinary people can start; ʿirfan begins at takhliya.
