# Weak final commas, Part Three (Ch 8–12): approval list (2026-10-20)

Per `tail-comma-plan-2026-10-19.md`. A weak final comma is a sentence that ends on a comma and a phrase with no verb of its own (an appositive, a participle phrase, or a tag). Not listed: the last item of a list; a quotation's attribution; a tail that is a full clause with its own verb.

Fix menu (the author's order of preference): (1) join with "and", or fold the tail in by removing the comma; (2) a which/who/whose clause; (3) move the phrase to the front or middle; (4) its own short sentence; (5) cut it if it only repeats.

Candidates: `voice.py punct` TAIL-COMMA/TAIL flags (false positives dropped, see the end), plus a read of each chapter's prose. Every **Before** occurs exactly once in its chapter file (checked); #37 is the one exception, noted there. No words inside quotation marks, citations, facts or theology are changed. Nothing has been edited yet.
