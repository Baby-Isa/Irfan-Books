- Headings 9.1-9.3 map one-to-one onto the brief's sections, in order. Each point box repeats the ladder: §1 steps 1-6, §2 steps 1-7, §3 steps 1-7. The body follows them in order; the Fudayl box sits at step 3, the young man at step 4, and Hatim at step 6, as the ladder places them.

- §2 step 1 ("the two meet") merges two statements instead of setting them side by side (D20); fix as above.
- The conclusion (l.144) follows: repentance open to anyone, kept steady, polishing kept by the law, starting with people. The Hatim sentence ("down to sparing an ordinary person's embarrassment") is the ladder's step 6.
- Hand-off present (l.146), Try this present, words box lists the four brief terms plus *tajliya*. Both story boxes and the unnamed prose story are as the brief (D47) directs.
