# Structure gap analysis (Fable): from about 4.3 to 5, 2026-10-07

**Recommended additions to the revised 17-chapter structure:**
1. **The Munajat Shaʿbaniyyah as a framing thread.** One line opens each part as an epigraph. Chapter 2 presents the whole prayer, and the epilogue closes on it. Every line must be checked against Mafatih.
2. **The Khoja devotional calendar as a secondary thread, in boxes.** Shaʿban, Ramadan, Dhu'l-Qaʿdah, Arafah, Muharram.
3. **"The living chain" moves.** It becomes the first chapter of Part Four, retitled "Finding a guide". Part Three is then three history chapters.
4. **Every chapter gets a scene-opener**, under 150 words, starting from familiar ground. Every chapter also gets a sourced "Try this" box. No practice is invented.
5. **Accessibility aids.**
   - A "Words you'll meet" strip at the head of each chapter.
   - The history is written as portraits: at most 5 figures per chapter in the prose, the rest in boxes.
   - Hand-off lines at the end of each part.
6. **Length.** A word budget of about 3.5–4.5k of body text per chapter, about 65k in total. Boxes are collapsible in the digital edition.
7. **Fidelity.**
   - The lecturer's voice is visible.
   - Each chapter gets a sources note in the back matter.
   - Karbala is offered as a reflection, grounded in Duʿa ʿArafah (held in Mafatih), not as the lecturer's teaching.
8. **The prologue states that irfan does not replace taqlid or the marjaʿ** (from L06).

**Rejected radical ideas:**
- Organising the book by the Khoja calendar. Its spine breaks the logical build-up.
- A fictional seeker. It invents experience.

**Projected scores:** A5 B5 C5 D5 E5 F5 G4.5 H4 I5.
- **H** cannot honestly reach 5, because about 25% of the book is not the lecturer's material by design.
- **G** could reach 5 only at about 50k words, which would cost lecture content.

**Open decisions:**
1. Whether to use the Munajat thread.
2. A body length of 65k or 50k.
3. Whether Karbala is stated as fact or offered as a reflection.
4. Whether boxes are collapsible.
5. Whether the lecturer reviews the "Finding a guide" chapter first.
