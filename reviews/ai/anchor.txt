Suggested fix: use the `seq_printf()` interfaces. They do the buffering. They handle reads of any size across packetized data. One buffer, filled once, flushed in a single pass.

The ticket costs a context switch. Who the f*ck does idiotic things like that? It isn't a performance bug. It's a decision, made once per byte, thousands of times, by someone who never stopped to ask what the loop was for.
