# Prologue and Chapters 1–3 sweep under TONE 46: before → after edits for approval (2026-10-19)

Approval sheet. Twenty-four edits across the prologue and Chapters 1–3, applying only TONE 46 (D51) to the prose; point notes and `<!-- src -->` comments are untouched. Each "before" is quoted exactly as it stands in the chapter file and occurs there once. Every "after" keeps the facts, quotations, citations, Arabic and logical step, and none ends on a trailing comma phrase. Not applied to the chapters.

The prologue and Chapter 1 are the approved exemplar, so only clear wins are proposed there (two and three). The author's choices on the Part Three sheet were followed: no "not" is cut where a reader might really have the thought, or where the lecturer or a source raises it; no story's own wording is changed; no quotation is moved; no "whereas" contrast is added; paragraph openers that restate for clarity stay; folds use "and", "which" or "since", never a trailing comma tail.

Kinds: **A** "not X" against a reading nobody raised → the positive statement · **B** short emphatic fragment folded into its neighbour · **C** mirrored closer, one half kept · **D** a flat sentence extended with a detail already in the chapter · **E** bald signpost worked into a sentence · **F** garbled lecture quotation paraphrased · **G** list of clauses with semicolons.

**Kind A, the full count.** Every "not" in the four files was checked against the transcript or the thought it answers. Two are proposed (13, 24). The rest stay. Prologue: "the two words do not say the same thing" (a premise), "not information about God" (the thought a reader really has, like the hunger line the author kept), "ends not in withdrawal from the world but in return" (the Part Five point), "not only knowing about God, but knowing Him" (the Chapter 2 point), "no separate sect or order", "does not go around the law or rise above it" (the fear the wary scholars raise), "what ʿirfan is and is not". Chapter 1: "This claim is not contested" (LINGO's own example), "not an invitation to circumvent the law" (raised by the wary scholars), "This is not limited to the careless" (L01 ¶8: "not even most pious people"), "not the same as perceiving it" (the section's step), "not of the colour and shape of the lamp" (Tabatabaʾi), "cannot be about the senses" (the reasoning from cattle), "it is not working", "It is not easy" (L01 ¶12: "not an easy path"), "not a shortage of evidence" and "God is not withholding signs" (point 4a), "one that is absent cannot be made fit" (the reasoning), "not about punishment at all" (the lecturer's contrast), "This does not mean turning away from the world" and "nothing wrong with pursuing material wealth" (L01 ¶25), "not about themselves but about God", "God is not far from anyone" (the thought "brought near" invites), "not unique to Islam" (L01 ¶3), "not about gaining power" (the lecturer), "powers are not the aim" (closing paragraph kept by the author). Chapter 2: "do not read it in a book or work it out from the clock" (sets up the chapter's contrast with study), "not only knowing about Him" (the chapter point), "does not come from outside" (defined against acquired knowledge), "cannot be wrong that they feel hungry" (L02 ¶32), "does not try to understand their hunger" and "not to define Him" (L02 ¶37), "what He is not", "cannot know God's essence", "not how much is known" (answers the "complete" of knowledge by presence), "nothing is held back" and "does not pick out some hearts" (the reading the paragraph itself raises), "not something gathered by study" (the section's conclusion), "The light is not absent while the heart waits" (answers the Sheikh's "wait for Allah"), "there is no maʿrifah", "cannot replace it" (the conclusion), "the light comes to the hearts of the pure, and darkness covers the impure" (L03 ¶19, the lecturer's own mirror, so not a kind C either).
