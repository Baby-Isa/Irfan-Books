# Fidelity check: Book 2, Chapter 5, "The soul and its worlds" (2026-10-18)

File checked: `book2/chapters/05-the-soul-and-its-worlds.md`. ## Verified clean

  - p. 21: "according to their true natures".
  - p. 75: "but to a lesser degree than waking".

  - p. 76: "call them 'Barzakh' or 'mithali'".
  - p. 77: "Sometimes rewards or punishments are nothing other than the actions..." (exact).
  - p. 77: "our future is now present".

- **Khomeini.** Both quotations are on p. 41 (the page runs from the p. 41 marker at line 1400 to the p. 42 marker at line 1438). "Seven sections... four" is on p. 32.
- **Other held sources.**
  - `light` p. 58 (Mutahhari's four terms) and p. 166 (epigraph English).

## Verdict

**PASS WITH FIXES.**

- All quotations and pages check out, including the two Shomali quotations at p. 75 and p. 76.
- The main fidelity risk is the "three worlds" framing (High).
- The chapter's own sentences that carry the most weight are the ones to soften: the barzakh box join, "adds to its form", the dhikr generalisation and the Qadi attribution.
