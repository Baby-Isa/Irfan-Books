## Specific checks

1. **D50 caution.** Present, once (l.43), but it comes after the claim is stated as fact (l.13, point box) and it covers only the stage placement. See the two medium rows for the fix. The wording that "the lectures do not place the five inside the four stages" needs the dhikr exception.
2. **L10 ¶44-47.** "Possible, but not desirable" is Tabatabaʾi's own verdict as the Sheikh reports it (¶45 "He said that it is possible, but it's not desirable"; ¶47 "Allama Tabatabaii says this is not very important"). The Sheikh agrees ("We are not taught to do this at home") but does not originate it. The chapter's side-by-side (Sheikh and Tabatabaʾi against the Kernel and the Lantern) fails: the Kernel is Tabatabaʾi's own programme, and neither the Kernel nor the Lantern says anything about forty days or any length. See the High row.
3. **Lantern p. 56 and Mafatih p. 802.**
   - Lantern p. 56 "way to rest": the four things are in the text but the Lantern says true rest comes only on meeting Allah (medium row).
   - Mafatih p. 802: paraphrase and quotation are exact. "According to validly reported traditions, the 'frequent remembrance' … indicates Tasbih al-Zahra'; therefore, whoever abides by it will have remembered Almighty Allah frequently". "Recited after each obligatory prayer" is in the Mafatih ("before he extends his legs after an obligatory prayer"). Counts 34 / 33 / 33 and the order are the "most famous method" of pp. 802-803 (low row about the variant order). The Sheikh's own figure (L10 ¶55: 34 times, "170 times Allahu Akbar every day") agrees with 34 × 5.

## Argument (D22) against the brief

- **Headings.** The four headings match brief sections 10.1-10.4 word for word. The conclusion is stated once, in the last section (l.179), and the approved hand-off follows.
- **Premises in order.** The prose follows the ladder, except that ladder §2 step 5 (the body's hold on the soul, Ch 5) has lost its pointer and its source (medium row).
- **Sourcing.** Every premise has a source except the three book-own mappings (stage placement, seclusion = separating, the doors frame) and the sleep bridge (l.94). Those are the places a hostile reader would attack.
- **Dependencies.** No step relies on later material. §3 uses the night prayer from §2 and Ibn Sina's steps from §1.
- **Does the conclusion follow?** Yes, apart from §4's side-by-side step, which asserts a disagreement the sources do not show (High row). With that step reduced to "the Sheikh, Tabatabaʾi, the Kernel and the Lantern all commend time alone, and the practice for life is the nightly hour", point 10.4 still follows and the "measured dose" conclusion stands.
