## Argument (D22) against the brief

- Three headings map one-to-one onto 11.1, 11.2, 11.3, in the brief's order. The chapter's body follows the ladder step by step (§1 steps 1-7, §2 steps 1-7, §3 steps 1-5); the Word Study, Reflection and Try-this boxes and the hand-off are in place.
- Dependencies hold. The epigraph is discussed straight after it; the Part One line is used before §2; "the verse above" in the Word Study follows 5:54. Nothing relies on a later step.
- Weak joints:
  - §3 step 2 (Saʿdi) supports "do not seek God's gifts instead of God", not "love is left". The conclusion "even love is left" therefore rests on Tabatabaʾi alone (`light` p. 95; `kernel` pp. 72-73); that is enough but should be stated as the single support, and Saʿdi framed as a related point (see Medium, l.155).
  - Rabiʿa in §3 is not needed for the step ("in this spirit" is loose).
  - The Tabatabaʾi text goes one stage further (the traveller "tries to forget love and the beloved", `kernel` p. 73, because even "lover and Beloved" is duality); the chapter stops at "only the Beloved remains", which is right for the hand-off to Chapter Twelve. Keep the word "tries".
- The conclusion follows if the two weak joints and the High fix are made.

**Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES** (1 High on the Imam al-Sadiq / Commander of the Faithful attribution at l.133; 14 Medium, of which the Amuli attribution, the 5:54 gloss, and the Kernel p. 70 overreach should be fixed before the PDF goes out).
