## Left as it was, on purpose
- "Abu Dharr did die alone in a faraway desert." A short sentence that lands a fact (TONE 46 keeps these).
- "Mutahhari names them in a single sentence." It carries a fact about the quotation and avoids a colon-then-quote in a chapter already above the exemplar's colon rate.
- The two SEMI-WEIGHT flags (edits 17 and 29): in both the second half explains the first, the pattern the user approved on 19 Oct.
- The remaining TAIL flag ("Sheikh Bahmanpour tells a story, reported by…, that shows…"): a normal relative clause, not a tail.
- The/This/That/It openers at 25%: most are "The picture's…", "The Imams'…", "The master of…", which name their referents; changing them would cost clarity (TONE 46, clarity wins ties).
