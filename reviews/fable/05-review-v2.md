# Review v2: Chapter Five, "The soul and its worlds" (2026-10-18, after the ladder rewrite)

Reviewer: Fable. Chapter not edited. Line numbers refer to `book2/chapters/05-the-soul-and-its-worlds.md` as reviewed. Checked against `docs/ladders/05.md` (user-approved), `docs/briefs/REWRITE-05-2026-10-18.md`, TONE 1, 25–27, 38–44, 40a, 44a, LESSONS 22–29, and the exemplar.

## Verdict

**Yes.** The ladder holds as a proof and the prose delivers every step in order; each nested run (§1 2a–c, §2 2a–d, §4 1a–c and 3a–b) is announced before it starts, and the four section points chain into the chapter point. A reader could read the first sentence of every paragraph and follow the argument. The single biggest remaining problem is a terminology clash inside §2's proof: step 1 (L74) defines the spirit as distinct from the soul, then steps 2b–2c (L86, L88) quote Shomali saying the *spirit* is connected to, and then disconnected from, the body. A careful reader asks which one leaves the body and has to read the run twice. Second: the chapter point's "by God's grace" rests on one sentence in the closing recap (L207) that nothing earlier argued and that raises a question the chapter does not answer ("once we have shown that we deserve it").

## 1. The ladder (point notes read alone)

No ladder edits. The ladder is the user's and the proof holds:

- §1: 1 (soul is creation in small, so its kinds of thing show creation's worlds) → 2 (three kinds, one per way of knowing) → 2a–c → 3 (so three worlds; images in the middle) → 4 (depths, not places) → 5 (Imam ʿAli: this world inside the next) → 6. Every key word of a later step ("worlds", "kinds of thing", "middle") appears in an earlier one. The user's objection from the first draft is answered: step 1 now says "each such kind makes up a world of its own" before "worlds" is used.
- §2 uses §1's "middle world" and "pure intellect and matter cannot meet"; §3 uses §2's "image body" and "movement between worlds"; §4 uses §3's "deeds shape the soul" and §2's "loosens the body's hold".
- One observation, not an edit: §3 step 1 carries two claims (angel fixed; soul not fixed, so shapeable). The prose rightly gives them one paragraph each around Q 37:164 (L114, L120), so the ladder could read "1. An angel has a fixed station (Q 37:164). 1b. The soul has none, so it can be shaped." Only worth doing if the user wants the note and the paragraphs to match one to one.
