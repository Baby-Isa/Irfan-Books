# Chapter Seventeen, "The return to God": Fable tone check (2026-11-01, Stage 4)

Compared with `book2/chapters/00-prologue.md`, `01-seeking-not-powers.md` and `02-knowing-about-not-knowing.md` ("Light in the heart" as the model of TONE 47), and against TONE 45–47, LINGO and LESSONS 30–37. Edits were applied directly in the chapter. Nothing inside quotation marks, verse or quote blocks, citations, Arabic or markers was changed; no fact or theology was changed. The §1 point note was renumbered to match the split paragraph.

## Verdict

The chapter is already in the house voice: concrete opening from the reader's life, one step per paragraph, verses that raise or answer a question, gentle wording on hell. The main faults were small and repeated: three weak final commas; four sentences that had to be read twice (a relative clause breaking the subject from its verb, a pronoun clash inside a quoted verse); a few stiff or abstract phrasings ("one's will … one wishes one has", "held against itself", "lies beyond our own level without intercession"); two sentences that restated the one before; and one quote (Q 89:27–28) discussed before it appeared instead of after. One paragraph was over the 110-word cap and two others sat at the cap. After the edits: profile PASS, longest paragraph 110, no TAIL-COMMA.
