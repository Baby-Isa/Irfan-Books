## Checks with no edit needed
- **Hand-off:** the last line matches the brief exactly: "These prayers keep asking God to work on the heart and the self. What is this self?"
- **Opener link to Ch 2:** now explicit (edit 2). The opener is about 125 words, within the brief's limit of 150.
- **Sourced additions beyond the brief (TONE 2):**
  - **Pass:**
    - Mafatih p. 129, "one of his closest disciples": it names the prayer in plain words (TONE 6).
    - Sahifa p. 8: it serves the heart-not-household point once the point is stated (edit 5).
    - journey p. 43, Ascending-Qur'an in the Word Study box: it sets up the §4 side-by-side.
  - **Fail, cut:**
    - light p. 156, the humility of the Imams (edit 16). The p. 156 familiarity quote is in the brief and stays.
    - Mafatih p. 245, "comprises excellent meanings" (edit 18).
    - Mafatih p. 129, Majlisi's "best of all supplications" (edit 19).
    - journey p. 43, "great favour to us" (edit 20).
- **Voice-judge tells:**
  - No sentence opens with And/But/Hence/So/Yet/Then once edit 1 is applied (checked with grep).
  - No claim+colon+quotation sentences.
  - No inverted openings.
  - Manner-commenting: only "he observes", which went with edit 16. "the Sheikh remarks" is neutral.
  - Balanced closers: edits 6, 7 and 14.
- **Quotations:**
  - Every displayed duʿa has its own plain introducing sentence and is spoken to after it.
  - Every displayed quote carries Arabic. The Lovers keeps its `[VERIFY]`.
  - The inline quotes (Abu Hamza §384, §599, §510) carry Arabic in `[…]{lang=ar}`.
  - §2 is still the densest stretch: four displayed quotes, one inline quote and Mutahhari in about 450 words. Each is spoken to, so it reads as a sequence rather than a wall. If the judge flags it, the first thing to cut is Mutahhari (p. 20). It makes the chapter's conclusion rather than §2's point.
- **Lingo:** no banned words, intensifiers or bad openers outside quotations. Prose has no "you" outside quotations and Try this.
- **Paragraph length (hand count):** the longest body paragraph is now about 85 words (§4 side-by-side). The average is about 55. The Word Study box (about 140 words) is in line with Ch 1's box.
- **"as given in the Mafatih"** appears three times. D41 requires the attribution, so it is kept.

## Verdict
1. Opener, link-back and hand-off now meet the brief. Each quotation is introduced plainly and spoken to, and four sourced additions that failed TONE 2 are cut.
2. The remaining voice risk is quote density in §2 and the paraphrase of the Sheikh's "primary school" line (edit 13), which the user may want restored as a quote.
3. Ready for `voice.py profile` (not run here; there was no shell), then the blind judge, then the fidelity check.
