## Findings and rationale, by criterion

### 1. Pyramid integrity
- **Thesis.** v1's thesis was a B1+B3+(part of B2) sentence. A reader checking it against the Parts would find Parts Four and Five unannounced. v2 names all five in the Part order, so the Prologue can state it and the Parts can be seen to deliver it.
- **MECE.** "It is safe within the shariʿa" (thesis) was argued in Ch 6 (Part Two) yet B3's question read "Is it ours, and is it sound?". One question per B: B2 keeps the boundary, B3 keeps lineage. B5 now says both returns, because Ch 17 (every soul's return to God) was not covered by "to people, with God, and finally to God Himself" as a *saints'* trajectory.
- **Conclusions.** Ch 16 v1 ("the path ends in return to people, with God") is, in the lecturer's own teaching, true only of the Prophet and the Imams (L09 ¶87–89). A conclusion that the lecturer would dispute cannot head a chapter in a book "heavily influenced" by him. v2 states what both sides accept (the completion is a return, shown fully in the Maʿsumin) and carries the disagreement in 16.3 with its `[QUERY]`.
- **Gaps.** Six claims the lectures press hard were absent: the Imams stand categorically above the mystics (L12 ¶37); the lecturer declines to judge Ibn ʿArabi (L13); techniques are secondary and each soul is its own channel (L14 ¶65–71, the lecture's distinctively Shia lesson); the journey is inward, veils not distance (L07 ¶4, L08 ¶3–9, the lecturer's definition of suluk); the disciplines have Quranic roots (L10 ¶11–12, ¶40); fana ends only the first journey (L09 ¶20, L15 ¶59). Each now sits where it does its work: 7.3, 8.3, 9.4, 11.1, 13.1, 15.3.
- **Surplus.** 6.3 "the whole route, in outline" did not support "no inner path outside the shariʿa". Rather than delete the map the outline wants, v2 makes it a premise: the route, even at its end, finishes among people under the law. 2.3 "so ʿirfan is not foreign" restated the conclusion; it is now a factual claim (these are the Mafatih prayers we recite) that the conclusion needs.
- **Tags.** Dual tags break the tree: a point cannot answer two parents. 2.3 anticipates B3 but serves B1; 10.1 reworded so its role in Ch 10 (guides exist to be found) is a B4 role.
- **Forward dependency.** None new. 6.3 previews the four journeys (OUTLINE dependency table already lists Ch 6 as their introducer).
