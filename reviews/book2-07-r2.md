**Chain of teachers (who taught whom)**

- Weaver to Shushtari to Hamadani; Hamadani to Karbalaʾi to Qadi to Tabatabaʾi (`light` p.106; `kernel` pp.8, 89). Shushtari attended Ansari's lectures and took over his chair for six months (`light` p.106, `kernel` pp.88-89). Qadi "two generations" below Hamadani is correct.
- Maliki Tabrizi: Najaf; fourteen years with Hamadani; Isfahani's letter; Qum; died 1925 (`journey` p.8 gives 11 Dhu'l-Hijja 1343 AH; `forty_hadith` p.8 gives 1304 Sh./1925). Maliki's ethics class resumed "three years after his death" by Shahabadi, who handed it to Khomeini in 1936 (`forty_hadith` p.8).
- Musabbihat and Qadi: L07 ¶15-16.
- Qadi's practice "half an hour or a little more": `light` p.107 and `kernel` p.89.

**L14 ¶8-9 "Imam" reading**: the plural reading holds: ¶8 "Aimamah" and "Imam Ali", ¶9 "a line of Imams", ¶10 "knowledge of the Aimmah". The chapter's "Imam ʿAli (as) received it as the Prophet's heir, not as one learned companion among others" is ¶8 almost word for word. The only unsupported parts are in H2. Tabatabaʾi's "his true successors" = the Imams is supported by the next paragraph of `kernel` p.80 ("the inner company of the Imam") and `light` p.100.

**Theology**: nothing found that says God is acted upon, limited or diminished. "Taught knowledge from God's own presence" is traditional and mild (see L1 for the translation point).
