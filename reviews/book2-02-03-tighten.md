## Ch2 reconciliation paragraph (hadith "whomever He wills" vs Q 24:35): verdict

- Stays careful on the main point. It does not say God picks and withholds, and it does not say the heart opens itself. The hadith wording is unchanged and still Khomeini's translation (p.331).
- The order is better than before: verse, then the reconciliation, then Khomeini, then Baqarah.
- The tightening weakened it in three ways. The Sheikh's explicit denial (L03 ¶20) is gone. The resolution is uncited. The two assertions that carry it ("every act is God's", "nothing happens except by His will") are the author's, not the Sheikh's or Khomeini's. See the first Med row for the fix.
- It remains consistent with the Sheikh. Closed heart (L03 ¶20), "no hindrance from the side of Allah" (¶21), "he doesn't choose you unless he loves you" (¶47) and the hadith as he quotes it (¶18, "whomever He wishes to guide") all fit the paragraph.
