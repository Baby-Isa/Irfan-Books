## Judgement calls made for you (please confirm or reverse)
1. **Ch 17, the told story.** The planned story (the Prophet's dream of ʿAbd al-Rahman ibn ʿAwf) rests on a report judged weak and shows a companion unfavourably, so Fable replaced it with the Sheikh's own Yusuf-and-the-king's-dream (L04 ¶81–83). The old wording is kept in `docs/ladders/17.md` if you want it back.
2. **Ch 17, the point's wording:** "ʿirfan is making that return willingly and in this life" (was "willingly, now"); the Part Five divider and ARGUMENT follow. Hell is described only as the lecture describes it (Khomeini's second reading cut); the lecture's "God doesn't want to revenge" sits beside its "will deserve the wrath of God", joined by the book's gloss "the wrath is what such a soul has made itself fit for, and not a change in God's mercy" (marked as a reading).
3. **Ch 16, Karbala.** The book chain you approved says the return runs "to Karbala", but no source says Karbala is the fourth journey, so the chapter marks it as the book's own reflection. The return's fullest form "belongs first to the Prophet and the Imams", with the Sheikh's opening of the third journey to "some very high caliber mystics" and Tabatabaʾi's "whoever God wishes" kept as stated. ʿArafah lines from §182, not the debated closing section.
4. **Ch 14, the critics.** No critic's own writing is among the held books, so the concerns are "heard through those who answer them" (said in one sentence; cut it if you prefer). "Pantheism" as a charge was unsourced and is replaced by the held charges (unbelief over the unity of being, Khomeini; Sadra called a heretic). The chapter point in the book chain still says "neither pantheism nor licence", which the chapter shows; keep or reword. The unity of being keeps one image (sea and waves); Imam ʿAli's oration was cut as a third authority on the same step. Bayazid: "He may have reached that further shore, but the river marks the state he preferred."
5. **Ch 15, authority.** Shia masters gave a shaykh real standing (Amuli took the robe and quotes "the Prophet, the Imam *or* the shaykh"; Tabatabaʾi's pledge; Khomeini as murid), set side by side; the chapter claims only "a teacher, yes; no chain of authority beside the Imams". The reading that the names in Duʿa Tawassul are the Kernel's "special teachers" is marked as the book's.
6. **Ch 13.** "At most some techniques were borrowed" became "may have been borrowed" (Baldick's hedge, not the Sheikh's). The *Lantern*'s authorship (Fudayl or Shaqiq) is set side by side. The Shaqiq story's line "Avoid much suspicion" is identified with Q 49:12 by the book.
7. **Epilogue.** "One year is enough" is given as the Sheikh's report (no held source). The practices are grouped in five short tables by time of day; each points back to its chapter.
8. **Book chain lines 13–17** in `docs/ladders/00-book-chain.md` now carry the chapters' final wording, marked "awaiting user approval". B4 and B5 are unchanged.
