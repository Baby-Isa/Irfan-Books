## Divine-action phrasing (checked)

No sentence says God is acted upon, limited or diminished. Phrases checked: "God places it in the heart"; "The lighting of any heart is God's act"; "nothing is held back on His side"; "open it to a light that was always there"; "waiting ... leaves the rest to God"; "A definition draws a limit, and God has none." Two need a qualifier so they cannot be misread: "bounteous by necessity" (M8) and the unqualified "complete" knowledge by presence applied to God (M7).
