## Verified, no change needed

- **Duʿa quotations.** English and Arabic match the extracts at the cited § for: Kumayl §221; Abu Hamza §384; ʿArafah §689, §781, §783-784; Munajat §82 and §92-93 (Arabic from `duas`, English from `light` p. 165) and §104 (Arabic from `duas`, English from `light` p. 165). Every block's English is word for word, with the final comma dropped where the extract has one. The Munajat §73-74 phrase, "except when You awakened me to Your love", is exact at `light` p. 164.
- **`light` p. 156** "You are already familiar with the words used in Imams' supplications" is exact, and the Shaʿbaniyyah lines follow it.
- **`mafatih`.** p. 129 (Kumayl; Thursday evenings and mid-Shaʿban nights; "one of the close disciples"). p. 243 ("confidential prayer"; Imam ʿAli and the Imams used to say it in Shaʿban; narrated by Ibn Khalawayh). p. 245 ("advisable to say it with presence of heart all the time" is exact). p. 282 (Abu Hamza; Zayn al-ʿAbidin; last hour of Ramadan nights). p. 374 (Imam al-Husayn; ʿArafat Day).
- **`journey`.** p. 43 "Descending-Qur'an" and "Ascending-Qur'an", and "is not accepted and comprehended by any one, except a small number of believers who are thoroughly familiar and enlightened", are verbatim. p. 111 Abu Hamza (see L4). p. 112 "scholar of vast learning who died in Najaf in 1359/1940" is exact.
- **`sahifa`.** p. 7 "the oldest prayer manual in Islamic sources" and "behind only the Qur'an and `Ali's Nahj al-balagha". p. 8 death in Medina 95/713-4 (or 94/712-3). p. 9 fifteen munajat or "whispered prayers", "added to several modern editions", Majlisi and *Bihar al-Anwar*. p. 656 Lovers, §2, "My God, place us with him whom Thou hast chosen for Thy nearness and Thy friendship", ellipsis placed correctly, "granted gazing upon Thy face". The Lovers is the ninth of the fifteen.
- **Q 6:103** (Qaraʾi): "The sights do not apprehend Him, yet He apprehends the sights". The chapter's "sight cannot perceive God" is a fair paraphrase.
- **Sheikh quotations.** "they have to cut off from everything first" is exact (L01 ¶20). "I recommend that you recite this in the month of Shaban as much as you can" is the source of the Try-this quotation (L01 ¶22). The primary-school line matches L01 ¶17 apart from the bracket (L6).
- **Citation keys.** `duas_dua_kumayl`, `duas_munajat_shabaniyah`, `duas_ramadan_dua_abu_hamza_thumali`, `duas_dua_arafah_imam_husain`, `light`, `mafatih`, `journey` and `sahifa` are all in `bibliography.bib`.
- **Honorifics and glossary spellings** are respected. "Whispered Prayer of the Lovers" and "Duʿa Abu Hamza al-Thumali" are used consistently.
- **Markers.** One `[VERIFY]` (M9). No `[QUERY]` or `[GAP]`.
- **Length.** About 2,700 words including boxes, under 3,600.
- **LINGO mechanical checks.** No banned words, intensifiers or "in order to" outside quotations. No sentence opens with So, Well, Look or Now. No jokes.

## Argument (D22 / brief)

- Headings map to the brief: opener, 3.1 (two sections), 3.2, 3.3 and conclusion. The order follows the brief.
- The conclusion follows from the premises. It holds because the chapter claims access and not attainment (l.154). The Mutahhari quote, Abu Hamza §510, §599 and the `light` p. 157 side-by-side were dropped from the brief; this removes sources but not steps.
- Weak points are premises 3.1-4 (M3, M4, support partly garbled) and 3.3-4 (M6, the Khomeini inference). Fix those before the conclusion is stated.

## Theology scan

No phrase says God is acted upon, limited or diminished. The nearest are M8 (distance placed on God's side) and L11 (the heart brought near "by its own passion"). Lines such as "He has already made it in the hearts of those He loves" (l.45) and "Even the turning towards God is His doing" correctly put the initiative on God.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH FIXES.** One High item (H1, a words-in-his-mouth error) must be fixed. The nine Mediums are mostly unmarked inference and one open marker. Before this goes to the user, fix H1 and M1-M8, and resolve the `[VERIFY]` (M9) before any `--final` build.
