## Namaz e Shab - Tahajjud Prayer - Salat al-Layl

_Description:_ Salatul-Layl consists up of <b>11 (eleven) Rak’ats</b><br><br>

The <b>first 8 (eight) Rak’ats</b> are prayed as normal <i>(like morning prayer)</i> in pairs of two Rak’ats each with the niyyah of <i>Nawafilatul-Layl</i><br>
The <b>next 2 (two) Rak’ats</b> are prayed (like morning prayer) with the <i>Niyyah of 'Salatul Shif’a</i><br>
The remaining <b>1 (one) Rak’at</b> is prayed (as below) with the <i>Niyyah of 'Salatul Witr</i>' as follows..<br><br>
After Niyyah and say Takbiratul Ihram Recite the following surah:-<br>
- <b>Surah Al-Hamd </b>- Once<br>
- <b>Surah Ikhlas (Qul Huwallahu Ahad)</b> - three times<br>
- <b>Surah Qul-Audhubi Rabbil Falaq</b> - once<br>
- <b>Surah Qul-Audhubi Rabbin Naas </b>- once<br>
Say Takbir and raise hands for Qunoot and You may recite <b>any Dua or Rabbana</b> or the following:

## Dua after each 2 Rakaat of Tahajjud Prayer - Salat al-Layl - Namaz e Shab

_Description:_ Supplication for his reappearance (may our souls be sacrificed for him) after every two (raka'at) units of the night recommended prayer
Source Mikyaal al-Makaarem, vol. 2, p. 14/26; Behaar al-Anwaar, vol.87, p. 250.

[§197]

EN: Have mercy on me in that dark house on my fear, my separation, and my loneliness have mercy on me

## Dua Hazeen to be recited after Salat al-Shab

[§216]
AR: اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ
EN: O Allah bless Muhammad and his family

[§217]
AR: اُنَاجِیْکَ یَا مَوْجُوْدًا فِیْ کُلِّ مَکَانٍ
EN: I whisper unto You O One Who is present in every place

[§218]
AR: لَعَلَّکَ تَسْمَعُ نِدَآئِیْ فَقَدْ عَظُمَ جُرْمِیْ وَ قَلَّ حَیَآئِیْ
EN: So that you may hear my call for surely my sin is excessive and my shame is less

[§219]
AR: مَوْلَایَ یَا مَوْلَایَ اَیَّ الْاَھْوَالِ اَتَذَکَّرُ
EN: My Master, O my Master which of the terrifying states shall I remember

[§220]
AR: وَ اَیَّهَا اَنْسٰی وَ لَوْ لَمْ یَکُنْ اِلاَّ الْمَوْتُ لَکَفٰی
EN: and which of them shall I forget for if there was nothing except death it would be enough

[§221]
AR: کَیْفَ وَمَا بَعْدَ الْمَوْتِ اَعْظَمُ وَ اَدْهَی
EN: then what about after death greater and much worse?

[§222]
AR: مَولاَیَ یَا مَوْلاَیَ حَتّٰی مَتٰی وَ اِلٰي مَتٰي اَقُوْلُ
EN: My master O my Master up to when and till when will I say,

[§223]
AR: لَکَ الْعُتْبٰی مَرَّةً بَعْدَ اُخْرٰی ثُمَّ لاَ تَجِدُ عِنْدِیْ صِدْقًا وَ لاَ وَفَآءً
EN: I am to blame, again and again, but then You do not find any truth or loyalty in me?

[§224]
AR: فَیَاغَوْثَاهُ ثُمَّ وَاغَوْثَاهُ بِکَ
EN: I call for help and I call for help,

[§225]
AR: یَا اَللّٰهُ مِنْ هَوًی قَدْ غَلَبَنِیْ وَ مِنْ عَدُوٍّ قَدِ اسْتَکْلَبَ عَلَیَّ
EN: O Allah from desires which have overpowered me and from the enemy which has pounced on me

[§226]
AR: وَ مِنْ دُنْیَا قَدْ تَزَیَّنَتْ لِیْ وَ مِنْ نَفْسٍ اَمَّارَةٍ بِالسُّوٓءِ اِلاَّ مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّیْ
EN: and from the world which attracts me and from the soul that leads towards evil except that on which my Lord has mercy (12:53)

[§227]
AR: مَوْلاَیَ یَا مَوْلاَیَ اِنْ کُنْتَ رَحِمْتَ مِثْلِی فَارْحَمْنِیْ
EN: My master O my master if You have had mercy on the likes of me then have mercy on me

[§228]
AR: وَ اِنْ کُنْتَ قَبِلْتَ مِثْلِیْ فَاقْبَلْنِیْ
EN: and if You have accepted from the likes of me then accept from me

[§229]
AR: یَا قَبِلَ السَّحَرَةِ اِقْبَلْنِیْ یَا مَنْ لَمْ اَزَلْ اَتَعَرَّفُ مِنْهُ الْحُسْنٰی
EN: O One Who accepts the early morning prayer accept me, O One who, I still know only good from Him

[§230]
AR: یَا مَنْ یُغَذِّیْنِیْ بِالنِّعَمِ صَبَاحًا وَ مَسَآءً اِرْحَمْنِیْ یَوْمَ اٰتِیْکَ فَرْدًا شَاخِصًا اِلَیْکَ
EN: O One who nourishes me with blessings morning and evening have mercy on me when I come to You alone,

[§231]
AR: بَصَرِیْ مُقَلَّدًا عَمَلِیْ قَدْ تَبَرَّءَ جَمِیْعُ الْخَلْقِ مِنِّیْ
EN: my glance fixed on You my actions carried on my neck When all of creation will withdraw away from me

[§232]
AR: نَعَمْ وَ اَبِیْ وَ اُمِّیْ وَ مَنْ کَانَ لَهُ کَدِّیْ وَ سَعْیِیْ
EN: yes, even my father and mother and those for whom I worked and struggled

[§233]
AR: فَاِنْ لَمْ تَرْحَمْنِیْ فَمَنْ یَرْحَمُنِیْ
EN: then if You will not have mercy on me who will have mercy on me

[§234]
AR: وَ مَنْ یُوْنِسُ فِیْ الْقَبْرِ وَحْشَتِیْ وَ مَنْ یُنْطِقُ لِسَانِیْ اِذَا خَلَوْتُ بِعَمَلِیْ
EN: who will give me solace from the loneliness of the grave and who will make me speak when I am alone with my deeds

[§235]
AR: وَ سَاءَلْتَنِیْ عَمَّا اَنْتَ اَعْلَمُ بِهِ مِنِّیْ
EN: and when You will ask me about what You know better than me?

[§236]
AR: فَاِنْ قُلْتُ نَعَمْ فَاَیْنَ الْمَحْرَبُ مِنْ عَدْلِکَ
EN: Then if I say yes (to my sins) where will be the escape from Your Justice?

[§237]
AR: وَ اِنْ قُلْتُ لَمْ اَفْعَلْ قُلْتَ اَلَمْ اَکُنِ الشَّاهِدَ عَلَیْکَ
EN: And if I say I did not commit it You will say was I not a witness over you?

[§238]
AR: فَعَفْوُکَ عَفْوُکَ یَا مَوْلاَیَ قَبْلَ سَرَابِیْلِ الْقَطِرَانِ
EN: So (I beseech) Your Forgiveness Your Pardon O my master before the wearing of the clothes of Hell

[§239]
AR: عَفْوُکَ عَفْوُکَ یَا مَوْلاَیَ قَبْلَ جَهَنَّمَ وَ النِّیْرَانِ
EN: Your Forgiveness Your Pardon O my Master before the Hell and the Fire

[§240]
AR: عَفْوُکَ عَفْوُکَ یَا مَوْلاَیَ قَبْلَ اَنْ تُغَلَّ الْاَیْدِیْ اِلٰی الْاَعْنَاقِ
EN: Your Forgiveness Your Forgiveness O my Master before the hands are tied to the necks
