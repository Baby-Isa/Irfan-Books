# The Prayer, Inside and Out: outline

A personal audio companion, for listening on long drives, on the outward
form and inner meaning of the daily prayer (salat) in its Ja'fari form.
Each track takes one stage of the prayer and explains what you say or do,
what it means, and what to hold in the heart at that moment.

Target: about 3 hours in total. The voice reads at roughly 185 words per
minute including pauses, so about 34,000 words across 13 tracks.

## Shape of every track

1. **Where we are**: one or two sentences placing this stage in the prayer.
2. **The outward form**: what is done or said, in the Ja'fari way, with
   the Arabic in transliteration followed straight away by its meaning.
3. **The words**: a word-by-word or phrase-by-phrase walk through, where
   there are words.
4. **The inner meaning**: what the sources say, in plain English, with
   short quotations and a story where one exists.
5. **To carry into your next prayer**: one or two things to hold in the
   heart at exactly this moment of the prayer.
